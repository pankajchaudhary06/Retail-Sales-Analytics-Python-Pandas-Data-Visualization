# 📊 Retail Sales Analytics & Customer Insights

An end-to-end **sales data analysis project using Python, Pandas, and data visualization** to uncover sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, geographical performance, and product relationships.

The project analyzes **186K+ sales records** to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that can support **marketing optimization, advertising strategies, product bundling, inventory planning, and revenue growth**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to analyze historical sales data and answer key business questions:

1. 📅 **Which month generated the highest sales, and how much revenue was generated?**
2. 🌎 **Which city generated the highest sales?**
3. 📢 **What time of day is most effective for advertising?**
4. 🛒 **Which products are most frequently purchased together?**
5. 📦 **Which products sell the most, and what factors may explain their popularity?**

---

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **186,849 records and 6 columns** representing customer transactions.

| Column               | Description                      |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Order ID**         | Unique identifier for each order |
| **Product**          | Product purchased                |
| **Quantity Ordered** | Number of units purchased        |
| **Price Each**       | Price per unit                   |
| **Order Date**       | Date and time of purchase        |
| **Purchase Address** | Customer delivery address        |

After data cleaning, **186,305 valid records** were available for analysis.

---

## 🔍 Analysis & Key Insights

### 📈 1. December — Highest Revenue Month

**December generated the highest monthly revenue of $4.61M.**

This significant increase suggests strong seasonal purchasing behavior, potentially influenced by the holiday shopping season, festivals, and increased gift purchases.

**Business Recommendation:**
Businesses can leverage seasonal campaigns, promotional offers, and increased inventory during the holiday period to maximize revenue.

---

### 🌎 2. San Francisco — Leading Sales City

**San Francisco recorded the highest sales among the analyzed cities.**

The city's strong technology ecosystem and consumer demand for electronic products may contribute to its strong sales performance.

**Business Recommendation:**
High-performing cities can be prioritized for targeted marketing campaigns, localized promotions, and optimized inventory allocation.

---

### ⏰ 3. Optimal Advertising Times

The analysis identified **10–11 AM and 6–8 PM** as high-performing sales periods.

These time windows may correspond to customer breaks during the day and post-work browsing hours.

**Business Recommendation:**
Advertising budgets can be concentrated around these high-performing periods to potentially improve customer engagement and conversion opportunities.

---

### 🛍️ 4. Frequently Purchased Product Combinations

**Phones and Charging Cables** were among the most frequently purchased product combinations.

This indicates a strong relationship between complementary products.

**Business Recommendation:**
Businesses can introduce:

* Product bundles
* Cross-selling recommendations
* Bundle discounts
* "Frequently Bought Together" offers

These strategies can increase average order value and improve the customer purchasing experience.

---

### 🔋 5. Top-Selling Products

The highest-selling products included:

* **AAA Batteries (4-pack)**
* **AA Batteries (4-pack)**
* **USB-C Charging Cable**

These products are likely popular because of their affordability, everyday utility, compatibility with multiple devices, and frequent replacement requirements.

**Business Recommendation:**
Maintaining sufficient inventory of high-demand products and using them in promotional campaigns can help reduce stockouts and maximize sales opportunities.
